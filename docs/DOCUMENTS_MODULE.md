# Documents Module

The module foundation is prepared in Stage 6 Phase 6A. Metadata supports customer documents, company documents, and company documents optionally associated with a branch. Customer/company pairs must be consistent, and archived entities cannot be selected for new active documents.

`organization_document_types` remains the existing extensible source of stable document types. The planned initial labels are Passport, UAE Visa, Emirates ID, Health Insurance, Labour Card, Work Permit, Driving Licence, Trade Licence, Establishment Card, Immigration Card, VAT Certificate, Corporate Tax Certificate, Tenancy Contract, Insurance, Municipality Approval, and Other.

Document UI, direct upload controls, preview/download controls, version history, and archive controls are Phase 6B work after the manual checkpoint. A 30-day expiry presentation window is defined, but reminders are not implemented.
